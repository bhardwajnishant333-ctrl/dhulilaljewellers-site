Purity explainedWhat 24K, 22K and 18K really mean.

Karat measures how much of the metal is pure gold, out of 24 parts. The rest is alloy (silver, copper, zinc) that gives strength and colour.

KaratFinenessPure goldBest for
24K99999.9%Coins, bars, investment
22K91691.6%Bridal and traditional jewellery
18K75075%Diamond and stone settings
Understand your billKnow what you are paying for, line by line.

Every jewellery bill has four parts: the gold value (weight × that day's rate), the making charge, the value of any stones, and 3% GST on the total. Try it in the bill decoder below.

Open the bill decoder
BIS Hallmark & HUIDHow to check that your gold is what it says.

Hallmarked gold carries three marks: the BIS logo, the purity grade (such as 22K916), and a 6-character HUID code that is unique to that piece. You can verify any HUID yourself in the government's BIS Care app.

Care for your jewellerySimple steps for lasting brilliance.

Put jewellery on last, after perfume and cosmetics. Store each piece separately in soft cloth. Wipe gold with a dry muslin after wearing. Keep kundan and meenakari away from water. Bring heirloom pieces to us once a year for a check of settings and clasps.

Certifications & Authenticity

bis.gov.inBIS Care appVerify an IGI report

  • BIS HallmarkCertifies the specified purity of precious metal
  • HUIDHUIDSix-character code that identifies and verifies each hallmarked article
  • 22K · 91622K · 916Fineness mark for 22 carat gold
  • 18K · 75018K · 750Fineness mark for 18 carat gold
  • IGIGraded diamonds, only where a valid IGI report supports the stone

A BIS hallmark certifies specified precious-metal purity. The HUID identifies the hallmarked article and lets it be verified. An IGI grading report applies to the specific diamond or jewellery it examined, not to every piece in the showroom.
